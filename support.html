A little help.

Making a delivery

Tap a neighboring square to move. Collect the parcel, then reach the door. Moss is silent, paths make 1 sound, leaves 3, and twigs 4. Carrying adds 1 except on moss. Sound fades over distance; hedges block it. Each step settles neighbors by 1 mark before new sound arrives. Three marks wake them.

Stuck?

Undo as often as you like. Listen previews a step before you take it. Hint outlines the first step of a quiet route; if your current position has no route, undo or restart. Neither uses currency.

Chapters and postmarks

Finish any 30 of a chapter’s 50 routes to open the next. Earn one postmark for delivery, two within two steps of the shortest route, and three for the shortest route. Today’s detour rotates at midnight UTC from a separate set of 365 routes; there is no streak to lose.

Progress and backups

Your journal saves on this device after each step. Before deleting the app or changing devices, open Settings and export a backup to Files. Importing replaces the current journal. There is no automatic cross-device sync.

Sound and accessibility

The game respects the silent switch. Settings includes sound, music/effects volume and haptics controls. Movement respects Reduce Motion. Tiles have VoiceOver descriptions including their position and sound risk.
