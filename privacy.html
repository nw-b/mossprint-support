Privacy policy · September 4, 2026

The app

Mossprint does not collect or transmit personal data. It contains no advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, account system, location access, or developer-operated backend. All levels, artwork and audio are bundled with the app.

Your journal

Progress and preferences stay on your device. An exported journal contains route numbers, steps and completion records. You choose where to save or share it. Apple’s device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Apple services

App distribution, purchases and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are governed by Apple’s policies. TestFlight can provide Apple-managed beta feedback and crash reports. Mossprint does not include a separate crash-reporting service.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer and investigate your request. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. This static website has no analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard connection logs under GitHub’s privacy statement.
